SiaMeal Snap

Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Published by James Patrick De Mesa

  • We collect what the app needs to track your nutrition: your account, profile, and the meals you log.
  • Meal photos, meal descriptions, and coach messages are sent to an AI provider to estimate nutrition and write replies.
  • No ads, no analytics trackers, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from inside the app.

What we collect

DataWhyWhere it's stored
Email address and password, or your Google sign-in To create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Our server (Supabase)

We don't use advertising or analytics SDKs. Reminders are scheduled on your device, so we don't collect a push notification token.

AI processing

When you scan or describe a meal, the photo or text is sent to our server and then to Google's Gemini API to estimate its nutrition. If Gemini is unavailable, OpenAI's API is used instead.

When you chat with Sia, your recent messages are sent along with the context Sia needs to give relevant advice: your display name, goal, diet type, daily targets, what you've eaten today, and the names of today's meals.

These providers process the data under their own terms: Gemini API terms and OpenAI privacy policy. AI estimates can be wrong, so please check them before you save.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, and photo storage.
  • Google (Gemini API) and OpenAI process meal photos, descriptions, and coach messages as described above.
  • Google also handles sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Open Food Facts receives the barcode number when you scan a packaged product. No personal information is sent with it.

These services may process data on servers outside your country. We share data with them only to run the features described here.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. When you do, your profile, meals, and photos are deleted from our server right away, and the app erases the data stored on your device. Copies may remain in our hosting provider's encrypted backups for a limited period before they expire.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit: change your profile and meals at any time in the app.
  • Delete: go to Profile → Delete Account. This is permanent.
  • Camera and photos: access is only requested when you use those features, and you can turn it off in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 or the GDPR may give you more rights, like getting a copy of your data. Contact us to use them.

Health information

SiaMeal Snap gives general nutrition information, not medical advice. If you're pregnant, managing a health condition, or have a history of disordered eating, please check your targets with a doctor or registered dietitian.

Children

SiaMeal Snap is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/SaucesCode/siaMeal-Snap/issues. Issues are public, so don't include personal details there. To delete your data, use Delete Account in the app.
