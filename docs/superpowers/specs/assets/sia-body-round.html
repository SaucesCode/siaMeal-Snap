Sia's body: a sitting Siamese in the same style as her head

Her head is unchanged and at its real size. The body follows the head's rules: flat colours (no gradients), the head's two line weights (6 for every outer edge, 5.5 for every inner line), a flat darker tone for shade (like her chin), hard-edged dark points on her legs, paws and tail (like her face mask), and cream toe lines (like her whiskers). Three builds, from round to slim.

Round, sitting, tail curled at her side

The tail comes from behind her, out to her right and curls up like a question mark. Lines on the body are a touch heavier so they read the same as her head.
